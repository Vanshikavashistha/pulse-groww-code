# Product pitch

*(100 words exactly)*

Pulse is an inbox for your portfolio, not a ticker. Most watchlists show
prices. Pulse stores where your attention stopped last time and shows only
what moved unusually since — measured in multiples of each symbol's own
volatility, not a flat percentage, because 2% in Maruti is news and 2% in
Adani is Tuesday. Discrete events are detected once and shared across users;
personal drift is measured from the price you actually saw. Marking as seen
advances a cursor the client rendered, so nothing detected mid-round-trip is
silently swallowed. Stale data is labelled, never hidden. If nothing happened,
it says so.
